# HCRP Development Team. Important Clarifications

Before starting your application or becoming part of the **Harbour Coast Roleplay Development Team** you need to read and agree to the following points. These rules are very clear. Apply to every person in the Development Team no matter what position they hold how much experience they have or how long they have been part of the team.

**1. Zero Tolerance Policy (ZTP)**

- The Development Team follows a *constant Zero Tolerance Policy (ZTP)**.

- **One serious mistake using your powers in the way or behaving badly can lead to being removed right away with no second chance.**

- There are no guarantees for warnings, more training or another chance. Our rules are strict and applied the way to everyone.

- Saying you did not know about a rule will not automatically make you not guilty.

**2. Authority & Responsibility**

- People in the Development Team have roles with trust, power and duties.

- Your access should only be used for development work.

- Using development tools, resources, admin access or any other powers for the reasons is not allowed.

- Your role does not protect you from following staff rules or facing consequences.

**3. Development Team Jurisdiction**

The Development Teams work is not about making games. It includes things like:

- Making games writing scripts creating buildings and handling tasks.

- Designing cars, uniforms, logos and other visual items.

- Planning projects, testing and putting things into action.

- Taking care of HCRP development resources.

- Looking at requests, changes and new ideas that come in.

- Keeping a level of quality across all approved development projects.

**4. Confidentiality & Asset Protection**

- Any games that're not yet ready private talks about development, files, scripts and resources must stay secret unless allowed to be shared.

- Sharing, copying, giving away selling or leaking development materials without permission is not allowed.

- You cannot change, move or use assets outside their approved use without getting the go-ahead.

- You must keep things even after you are no longer part of the team.

**5. Professionalism & Conduct**

- Everyone must act in a grown-up, professional, responsible and respectful way all the time.

- Disagreements, personal relationships or your rank should never affect the choices you make about development.

- Being careless not honest trying to stop things on purpose or using your power wrongly will not be accepted.

- Development Team members are expected to look good for HCRP at all times.

**6. Compliance & Accountability**

- Every person in the Development Team must follow all HCRP rules, development steps and the Roblox and Discord Terms of Service.

- Development Leadership can check your access look into behavior and take action when needed.

- You can lose your position or access if you do not meet the teams standards.

## Final Acknowledgement

By moving you say that you understand the **strict rules of the HCRP Development Team**, the power and duties that come with the job and the fact that our Zero Tolerance Policy is always, in effect.

**The Development Team is a position of trust, not a position of entitlement.**

**To go ahead please reply with:**

"I have read and understood all Development Team clarifications. I acknowledge the Zero Tolerance Policy understand that violations may result in removal without a second chance and agree to uphold all Development Team standards and responsibilities."
